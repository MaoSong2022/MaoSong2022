#### 🔭 Current Focus

I work on pushing the capability frontier of foundation models, with a current focus on **LLM Post-Training** and **Agentic RL** (algorithms, efficiency, engineering, and data). My broader interests span:

- **LLM Post-Training & RL** — RLHF, RLVR, OPD, and Agentic RL
- **Model Architecture & Scaling Laws** — Attention, FFN / MoE, and frontier lab technical reports
- **Multimodal LLMs (MLLMs)** — Vision encoders, document understanding, and unified architectures
- **Training Systems & Efficiency** — Distributed parallelism, FSDP, and train-inference alignment

#### 🌟 Selected Work & Open Source

- **ICLR 2026** — *Investigating Redundancy in Multimodal Large Language Models with Multiple Vision Encoders*  
  Systematically analyzes multi-encoder redundancy and challenges the assumption that adding more vision encoders always improves MLLM performance. ([Project Page](https://maosong.website/Encoder-Redundancy/))
- **DocGenome & DocParser** — *An Open Large-scale Scientific Document Benchmark for MLLMs*  
  Built a training-free LaTeX-based parsing pipeline ([DocParser](https://github.com/Alpha-Innovator/DocParser)) and released a 500K-paper benchmark ([DocGenome](https://github.com/Alpha-Innovator/DocGenome)) with structured layout, OCR, and logical relation annotations. ([Project Page](https://alpha-innovator.github.io/DocGenome_page/) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11633))
- **[astro-blog-template](https://github.com/MaoSong2022/astro-blog-template)**  
  An open-source Astro template for research articles, math-heavy tutorials, and technical blogs (powering [maosong.website](https://maosong.website)).
